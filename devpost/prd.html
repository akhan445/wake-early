Wake Early

Drink right. Sleep tight. Wake early.

A fun, encouraging app that connects caffeine → bedtime → wake-up in one place and celebrates completion instead of streaks.

From scope to product

Scope: “a view that connects them”
→
Three nested rings + a ☕ → 🛏 → ☀️ path bar on Home
Scope: “log a drink, see when it clears”
→
Search brand drinks, watch it pour, a red line that drops toward bedtime
Scope: “encouraging win messages”
→
A goal ladder of on-time wake-ups (1 → 2 → 4 → 7 → 30) that never resets
Scope: “solve a maze to turn it off”
→
Maze → a two-tap morning check-in → rings sparkle on Home

Screens, behavior & checks

Home, top to bottom

  1. Today’s rings (wake-up outer, caffeine, bedtime) + the path bar showing what’s left: “You’re up on time — keep caffeine under the line and be in bed by 11.”
  2. Goal ladder — on-time wake-ups: 1 → 2 → 4 → 7 → 30. Never resets.
  3. This week — “Under your caffeine line 4 days,” “In bed on time 3 days.”
  4. Week comparison — last week vs. this week as two growing columns.

Decisions, boundary & open questions

DECIDED Your product decisions
  • Completion over streaks — counting wins motivates; breaking a streak discourages.
  • Completion is the reward — no “build something” game for now.
  • Three nested rings + path bar — clearest, and shows they’re one whole.
  • A day is the calendar day — waking up on time is an immediate win; today propels tomorrow.
  • Check-in right after the maze — two pre-filled taps, one celebration moment.
  • “Going to bed” tap instead of phone-activity detection.
  • Ladder counts on-time wake-ups — the point of the app.
  • Moving red line tied to bedtime; pour animation; on-screen shake, not vibration; no faces.
ASSUMED Accepted at approval
  • “On time” = maze solved by the target wake time, no grace period.
  • Phased moves the alarm 15 minutes earlier per day.
  • The demo’s sample week loads through a “load sample week” option.
  • The 7–14-day trend was dropped in favor of the ladder, weekly counts, and week comparison.
DEFERRED Not in the POC
  • Phone-activity sleep detection · check-in notifications · sensor challenges · accounts/sync
  • Later: a “build something” reward, Four Tendencies, deeper sleep tracking, optional vibration
NON-GOALS
  • No AI · no streaks or badges · no faces · no medical advice
FOR 4-SPEC Open questions
  • Where the drink data comes from (library / dataset / API; fallback: hand-picked list)
  • Caffeine half-life and the “clear enough to sleep” amount
  • Caffeine page design and colors — you want to revisit these
  • How the alarm actually rings — phone app or browser?
