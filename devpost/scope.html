Wake-Up App (working title)

A fun, encouraging app that treats waking up early as a whole process — not just an alarm.

The unique kernel

Connect the whole chain

Caffeine affects when you can fall asleep; bedtime affects when you wake up. The app shows that chain in your own data instead of just ringing at you.

A game, not a grind

It celebrates wins and projects them forward — “15 minutes earlier today; keep it up for a week and you’ll be up two hours earlier.”

The core loop

Who it’s for

Someone — starting with you — who keeps meaning to wake up early but can’t make it stick, especially someone who finds focus and routines hard. Today they set an alarm and hope; they don’t see how caffeine and late bedtimes set up the failed morning.

“Things that will work for me, but also as an extension will work for anybody else.”

What “working” looks like — the demo

A new user walks through onboarding:

  • Picks their wake-up goal
  • Chooses cold turkey or phased
  • Sees a short explainer on how caffeine affects bedtime
  • Picks a maze difficulty — easy, medium, or hard

Skip ahead to a user who already has a week of data:

  • Log a drink → see when the caffeine will clear
  • View caffeine → bedtime → wake-time patterns, connected
  • Encouraging win messages and the phased schedule
  • Alarm goes off → solve the maze (top-left to bottom-right) to turn it off

The “oh, that’s cool” beat: your own caffeine, bedtime, and wake-up times connected, with a message celebrating how far you’ve come.

What’s in, what’s later, what’s cut

NOW The proof of concept
  • Onboarding: wake-up goal, cold turkey vs. phased, caffeine explainer, maze difficulty
  • Log a caffeinated drink and see when it clears (half-life)
  • A week of mock caffeine / bedtime / wake data, with a view that connects them
  • Phased schedule math: 10–15 min earlier a day, possibly from a baseline average
  • Encouraging win and projection messages
  • Wake-up maze challenge with three difficulty levels
LATER Worth doing, just not now
  • Real device alarms and sensor challenges (move the phone 15–20 ft, push-ups, shaking)
  • Tokens / “build something” game layer
  • Four Tendencies personality profiling (Upholder, Questioner, Obliger, Rebel)
  • Detecting wake-ups from phone activity — and telling a real wake-up from a 3 a.m. scroll
  • Deeper sleep tracking and bedtime reminders
CUT Named, with reasons
  • AI — the personalization this POC needs is simple math; you decided it isn’t necessary yet.
  • Sensor-based challenges — need native phone access and would eat the whole time budget; replaced by the maze.

Screen ideas (for the PRD)

☕ Caffeine cup

A cup fills with coffee as you log drinks, with a “don’t go past this mark” line. It drains through the day as the caffeine wears off.

🌙 Sleep/wake bars

Horizontal bars per night (bedtime → wake), with dashed lines marking your target window, e.g. 11 pm–6 am. You can see yourself fitting into it.

Why it matters to you

Sleeping and waking on time is a real problem for you, and you want to build around how different people actually work.
